Top Drive

Detailed operational knowledge of Drillmec hydraulic top drive mechanical assemblies, hydraulic actuation and control circuits, gear-dependent torque/power characteristics and operating limits. Troubleshooting includes recurrent faults, indications of misuse versus fatigue-related damage, failure severity and operational consequences. Experience planning and supervising routine servicing and major mechanical repairs, including approved interventions with the top drive installed in the mast. Daily washpipe lubrication checks are supported by digital rotation-hour records derived from drilling data.

Drilling execution & equipment reliability

Accountability for the integrity, readiness and reliability of complete onshore drilling systems. Operational supervision spans well-programme execution, hole cleaning, drilling hydraulics, solids control, directional drilling, casing and cementing interfaces, and well-control preparedness.

Equipment assurance combines planned maintenance, running-hour monitoring, fault diagnosis, critical-spares management, inspection programmes, API Level IV coordination and controlled return to service. Technical troubleshooting is conducted through approved procedures, multidisciplinary maintenance support and evaluation of operational risk.

Safety leadership: Work within ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 management systems, with risk assessments, Permit to Work, isolation/LOTO, HSEQ inspections, behavioural audits, competency development and emergency preparedness. Personnel safety, well integrity and environmental protection take precedence over production targets.

Supporting evidence: client KPI targets, casing drawings, contractual BHA requirements, HSEQ procedures, training programmes and scheduled inspection plans in the project gallery.

Operational records & performance

Structured control of productive time, NPT and downtime, drilling parameters and equipment operating hours, supported by PASON/NOV EDR data and daily operational reporting. Performance is reviewed against contractual KPIs, programme targets and the actual conditions encountered at the wellsite.

Cost management connects resource allocation, personnel and contractor mobilisation, fuel and water consumption, spare parts, logistics and maintenance expenditure with budgets, forecasts and corrective action plans. Microsoft Project schedules and Power BI S-curves support planned-versus-actual analysis, schedule deviation assessment and transparent reporting to clients and management.

Management approach: Translate operating records into actionable decisions: identify recurring failures, prioritise reliability improvements, investigate deviations, document follow-up actions and coordinate rig crews, maintenance, HSEQ and client representatives.

Supporting evidence: contractual KPI table, planned inspection schedules, behavioural-audit programme, superintendent field-visit plan and original planning/performance documents in the gallery. Additional verified charts and field records can be added without altering this layout.
